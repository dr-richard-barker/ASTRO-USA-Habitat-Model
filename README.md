# ASTRO-USA Habitat Model

A parametric model of the ASTRO-USA **Mini-Hab Food Production Module (FPM)**, the shipping container described in
Peters et al., *Analog Simulation Training and Research Outpost Utilizing Self-sustaining Architecture: A Testbed for
Moon and Mars Technology at Purdue University*, 55th International Conference on Environmental Systems, ICES-2026-251.

One sourced **ledger** (`ledger/*.yaml`) of dimensions and loads drives every output. It refuses any value that has
neither a source (with a verbatim quote) nor a declared assumption.

1. **Engineering CAD**: build123d → STEP / STL / GLB, plus dimensioned plan and elevation drawings.
2. **CFD**: OpenFOAM v2606 in a pinned Docker image, covering air, heat, CO₂, water vapour and age of air, with mass,
   energy, species and age-of-air balance gates.
3. **VR**: Blender-built LOD scenes (GLB, USD/USDZ, FBX) and a browser WebXR viewer with an optional multi-user
   relay.

## What is in it

- **FPM floor plan from the paper's vector Figure 2.** All 22 printed dimensions agree with their own dimension lines
  within 0.1 in (`sources/fig2_geometry.json`). The CAD is gated against the extracted plan (`habitat/checks/fig2_overlay.py`).
- **Container shell from a manufacturer specification** (SeaCube 40′ high-cube); crew loads from NASA BVAD Rev2.
- **Generic bioreactor payloads in the bioreactor bay, as envelopes only.** Outer boxes, gas ports and loads for an
  unbranded ten-vessel bioreactor rack, a photobioreactor, an incubator and a sensor module (`ledger/payloads.yaml`,
  `ledger/payload_ports.json`). All are declared placeholders, to be replaced once the team chooses the hardware.
- **CFD cases** generated from the same registry (`habitat/cfd/`): the M0 baseline and the S6 payload-siting study,
  which places three CO₂ route tracers in one flow field.
  - The steady runs did not close their balances (the flow is unsteady), so the cases are re-run as time-averaged
    transients. The mesh-convergence (GCI) study uses a 113 / 80 / 57 mm ladder.
  - Results land in `results/cfd/` only when a run passes its gates. The first coarse steady attempts and their
    failing gates are committed there as `fpm_m0_coarse_*`.
- **VR scenes** (`make vr`): procedural, regenerated (not decimated) at three levels of detail, with per-node
  metadata, a public element allow-list and a leak test. Import notes for engines: [vr/engine_pack](vr/engine_pack/README.md).
- **Viewer** (`vr/web/`, three.js): layer toggles, cut-away, CFD overlays, payload port details and WebXR. It has
  only been tested on desktop; no headset has been tried yet. Multi-user presence and review pins use the relay in
  [relay/](relay/README.md), which runs locally and is not deployed.
- **Side studies:**
  - a light-budget check: can the stated power light the NFT wall into NASA's lettuce crop-model range?
    ([results/light_budget.md](results/light_budget.md));
  - S3 HVAC off, door closed: a zero-dimensional CO₂ build-up model against the OSHA limit
    ([results/s3_hvac_off.md](results/s3_hvac_off.md));
  - a CFD-informed sensor placement ranking for the team's monitoring (`habitat/cfd/sensors.py`).
- **Concept level (labelled as such everywhere):**
  - the rest of the Mini-Hab site after the paper's Figure 10, with Laboratory and Crew Quarters modules and a
    BioWaTERR trailer placeholder;
  - a primary-habitat concept generator compared with NASA's net-habitable-volume guideline
    ([results/primary_concept.md](results/primary_concept.md)).

  Neither is the team's design.
- **Project page**: `docs/` (generated by `make site` from `site/index.template.html`): the habitat, the analysis
  results, the ASTRO-USA concepts and the interactive 3-D model. Every number is a placeholder filled from the
  result files, and the tables are the manuscript's tables, so the page and the paper cannot drift. To preview it
  locally, serve `docs/` with any static server (`python3 -m http.server -d docs`). It is not yet published: GitHub
  Pages will be switched on once the ASTRO-USA team has agreed to a public release.
- **Manuscript draft** (`manuscript/`, `make manuscript`): every number is a macro generated from the result files
  (`habitat/manuscript_numbers.py`); introduction, discussion and author statements are left to the authors.

A one-page summary for the ASTRO-USA team lead, generated from the results, is in [TEAM_BRIEF.md](TEAM_BRIEF.md).

Everything not stated in a source is an explicit, listed assumption: see [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md). The facts
we need from the ASTRO-USA team are in [TEAM_INPUTS.md](TEAM_INPUTS.md). Progress, open items and how to resume are
in [STATE.md](STATE.md); decisions and risks are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) and [RISKS.md](RISKS.md).

## Reproduce

```bash
uv sync
make cad check docs test        # CAD, Figure 2 overlay gate, generated docs, tests
make vr site                    # VR scenes (needs Blender 5.2), then drawings, figures and the project page
make cfd-coarse post            # needs Docker (pulls the pinned OpenFOAM v2606 image)
```

`make fig2` re-extracts the floor plan and needs the paper PDF in `sources/private/`. The PDF is not distributed
here; the paper and slides belong to the ICES-2026-251 authors.

## Sources

- ICES-2026-251 (team paper): footprint, zone layout, equipment counts, electrical loads.
- SeaCube Containers, 40′ High Cube Dry and 20′ Standard Dry specification sheets: container dimensions.
- NASA/TP-2015-218570/REV2, *Life Support Baseline Values and Assumptions Document* (BVAD, 2022), Table 3-26: crew
  metabolic heat, CO₂ and water vapour.
- BVAD Rev2 Tables 4-111 and Section 4.14.1 (Modified Energy Cascade): lettuce planting density, photoperiod and
  the light range of NASA's crop model (used by the light-budget check, `results/light_budget.md`).
- Kusuma, Pattison & Bugbee (2020), *Horticulture Research* 7:56, doi:10.1038/s41438-020-0283-7: achieved LED
  fixture efficacy.
- Whitmire et al. (2014), NASA JSC-CN-32284 (NTRS 20140016951): minimum acceptable net habitable volume.
- OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1000 Table Z-1 (via eCFR): CO₂ permissible exposure limit. NOAA GML global monthly mean CO₂.
- CoolProp: air properties.
